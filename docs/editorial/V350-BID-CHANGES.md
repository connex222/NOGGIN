# Bid to Win — v3.5.0 category changes (Q7)

Open categories now accept 40+ answers; anything unlisted is judged by the TV or a player who is not in the duel.
Every category is flagged `review: true` until you have checked it (see RELEASE-REVIEW-QUEUE.csv).
