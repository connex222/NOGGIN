# NOGGIN host voice — recording script

NOGGIN host voice. Record each line as public/voice/<id>.mp3 (extra takes: <id>-2.mp3, <id>-3.mp3). Any line you skip simply stays silent. Then upload them; the server finds them automatically

**Recorded so far: 0 of 36 lines.** Tick them off as you go.

Tips: record on your phone in a quiet room, about 20 cm from your mouth. Big energy — it's a game show. Leave half a second of silence at each end. Any of the suggested wordings is fine, or say it your own way.
