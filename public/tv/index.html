HOW TO PLAY NOGGIN

  1. Leave NOGGIN on your TV or computer.
  2. Each player scans the QR code on their phone, picks a contestant and taps READY.
  3. Once everyone is ready, the TV host starts the show.
  4. Roll, choose your route, win mini-games and collect Sparks. First to the chosen target wins!

Change music, sound effects and game length in Settings before the show starts.
