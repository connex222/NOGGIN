HOW TO PLAY NOGGIN

  1. Leave this screen on your TV or computer.
  2. Each player scans the QR code with their phone, picks a contestant and taps READY.
  3. Once everyone is ready, the TV host starts the show.
  4. In Classic: roll, move, play mini-games, and collect Sparks. In Showtime: add Director Cues, landing choices, bets and surprise twists. First to the chosen Spark target wins!

Use the TV lobby to select Classic (default) or Showtime, timer speed and game length before starting. Sound and music controls are at the top.
