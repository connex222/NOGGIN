The original archive contained `spinopel-spinning-roulette-wheel-429832.mp3`, a filename resembling a downloaded sound effect. It was unreferenced and has been removed from the playable game, alongside the unreferenced `sfx-hop .mp3`. **That resemblance is not proof of its origin.** The other recordings cannot be assumed original solely from their filenames or encoding.

For every retained recording, record the source URL or production project, original creator, licence name, acquisition date, and proof of permission for redistribution and commercial use. Replace any recording without proven rights with newly produced or properly licensed audio.
